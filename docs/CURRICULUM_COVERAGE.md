# Curriculum coverage

All entries are planned, not complete. Coverage is based on the user's specification; an authored lesson must have objectives, Roman Urdu explanations, rules, examples, vocabulary meanings, practice/assessment and revision metadata. Language review must be recorded separately from automated structural validation. No CEFR certification is claimed.

## Quranic Arabic
No Quranic material is approved or bundled yet. Source/license acquisition, exact text/reference/word alignment, frequency method and qualified linguistic/religious review are prerequisites. Vocabulary recognition must never be labelled full ayah comprehension.

## Exercise coverage
Planned: single/multi-choice, fill blanks, ordering, matching, listening, dictation, translation, writing, pronunciation repetition and guided speaking. Deterministic activities need real validation/persisted attempts; open-ended work needs transparent rubrics and explicit uncertainty. Do not substitute a vocabulary quiz for the full skill checklist.
